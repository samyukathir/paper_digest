## [Performance, Generalizability, and Fairness of a Peripheral Artery Disease Detection Model Across Patient Phenotypes and Health Systems](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.19.26360861)
**High** · medrxiv · 2026-08-22

*Why it matters: Directly addresses your interests in AI/ML applied to healthcare EHR data and clinical decision support, with emphasis on fairness, generalization, and phenotypic analysis across diverse populations.*

## [A Neurosymbolic Approach for Constructing Planning Domain Models from Clinical Narratives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21186v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-08-21

A neurosymbolic framework (NSPIN) combining LLMs with symbolic planning methods to extract structured event sequences from clinical narratives and induce probabilistic planning domain models for surgical workflows, evaluated on laparoscopic appendectomy notes.

*Why it matters: Combines LLMs with reasoning methods for clinical NLP, extracting structured knowledge from unstructured clinical text for decision support in high-stakes surgical contexts.*

## [Causal Modeling of Adverse Pregnancy Outcomes via Adaptive LLM Proposals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21079v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-08-21

A neurosymbolic framework that iteratively uses LLMs as adaptive proposal distributions to generate plausible causal hypotheses for adverse pregnancy outcomes, scoring against empirical clinical data to identify disease-driving mechanisms.

*Why it matters: Applies LLMs combined with causal reasoning to clinical data analysis, demonstrating methods for knowledge discovery and hypothesis generation in healthcare applications.*

## [Designing a Robust LLM-Based Evaluation System for Agentic AI in Drug Discovery Through Human Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21057v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-08-21

Presents an LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation framework for agentic LLM systems in drug discovery, with human alignment study validating four quality dimensions and optimizing judge performance through few-shot learning.

*Why it matters: Addresses evaluation and reasoning methods for LLM-based AI systems applied to scientific/biomedical workflows, combining deep learning with human alignment for drug discovery applications.*

## [A Modular Agent for Reliable and Auditable Spatial Relation Verification in CT Scans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21140v1)

*Why it matters: Demonstrates interpretable deep learning architecture for medical image analysis and clinical NLP, combining vision models with structured reasoning for radiological understanding.*

## [Delta Marches: Generative AI based image synthesis to decode disease-driving morphologic transformations.](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.03.18.643999)
**High** · biorxiv · 2026-08-22
