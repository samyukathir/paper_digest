## [ResidencyRL: Reinforcement Learning in Simulated Clinical Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07418v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-08-07

ResidencyRL uses reinforcement learning to train clinical AI agents through simulated multi-turn patient encounters, improving diagnostic accuracy by 7% and reducing missed red flags by 31% compared to base LLM models.

*Why it matters: Directly addresses AI/ML methods for clinical decision support and reasoning in healthcare, combining LLMs with RL for medical applications.*

**High** · arxiv · 2026-08-07

*Why it matters: Addresses AI/ML reasoning and evaluation methods with application to scientific analysis in healthcare and biology, demonstrating improved reliability through specialized training.*
