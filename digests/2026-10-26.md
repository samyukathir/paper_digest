## [Deep Learning-Based Prediction of HER2 Status from Whole-Brain MRI in Breast Cancer Brain Metastases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32774v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-10-23

Uses deep learning (fully convolutional networks) to predict HER2 status from whole-brain MRI scans in breast cancer brain metastases, comparing radiomics, segmentation-free, and mask-guided approaches.

*Why it matters: Directly applies deep learning architectures to medical imaging analysis and biomarker prediction from clinical images, combining computational pathology principles with healthcare AI.*

## [Beyond Discrete Tokens: Preserving Numerical Continuity in Medical Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32948v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-10-23

Develops HAD-VLM, a hybrid autoregressive-diffusion vision-language model for quantitative ultrasound measurements that preserves numerical continuity rather than using discrete tokens.

*Why it matters: Demonstrates novel deep learning architecture combining vision-language models with medical imaging applications, addressing quantitative measurement in clinical ultrasound and radiology.*

## [Serum metabolic characteristics and potential biomarkers of first-episode schizophrenia: An exploratory machine learning-based metabolomics study.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pnpbp.2026.111984)
**High** · europe_pmc · 2026-10-24

Applies machine learning to identify metabolic biomarkers in first-episode schizophrenia using serum metabolomics data, employing Bayesian networks and WGCNA for biomarker discovery.

*Why it matters: Uses ML/deep learning methods for biomarker discovery in omics data (metabolomics), demonstrating computational approaches to identifying disease signatures relevant to clinical applications.*

## [A proteomic polygenic score to identify IL-18 driven inflammatory bowel disease](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.18.26353508)
**Medium** · medrxiv · 2026-10-24

Develops a 27-SNP polygenic score using proteomics data from 50,000+ individuals to predict IL-18 levels and identify IBD patients with IL-18-driven disease for targeted therapeutics.

*Why it matters: Applies AI/ML polygenic methods to genomic and proteomics data for patient stratification and precision medicine in inflammatory bowel disease.*

*Why it matters: Addresses AI agents for autonomous biology research with applications to experimental design and bioinformatics, relevant to AI methods applied to biological discovery systems.*
