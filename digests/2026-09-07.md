## [MARLA: An Autonomous Agent for Medical AI Research and Development](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.21.26361049)
**High** · medrxiv · 2026-09-05

MARLA is an agentic framework that enables clinicians to autonomously conduct complex multimodal medical AI research by automatically translating high-level research goals into executable hierarchical research loops, coordinating specialized code agents, and iteratively refining strategies based on experimental feedback.

*Why it matters: Directly addresses autonomous AI agents for medical research automation, combining LLMs with medical AI development workflows across multimodal data.*

## [A Structured Debate-Mixture-of-Agents Framework for Complex Clinical Diagnostic Decision Support](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05069v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-09-04

Debate-Mixture-of-Agents (DMoA) is a multi-agent LLM framework that structures role-based interaction for iterative diagnostic reasoning, improving diagnostic accuracy by 10.21 percentage points and safety by 11.36 percentage points on rare disease and challenging clinical cases.

*Why it matters: Presents novel LLM reasoning architecture for clinical decision support with structured multi-agent frameworks, directly relevant to healthcare AI applications.*

## [Constructing and Evaluating Clinical Reasoning Trajectories for Medical Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05090v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-09-04

MedTraj proposes a framework for evaluating medical AI agents by treating reasoning trajectories as critical objects, assessing quality across coherence, evidence support, hallucination, completeness, and traceability dimensions using error injection and step-level filtering.

*Why it matters: Develops evaluation methods for medical LLM reasoning quality beyond answer correctness, essential for clinical AI safety and decision support.*
