# Paper Digest — 2026-10-05

## [Transcriptome-informed multi-modal AI for predicting neoadjuvant therapy response from breast cancer biopsies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03693v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-10-02

A two-stage deep learning model that infers transcriptome information from histopathology images to predict pathological complete response to neoadjuvant therapy in breast cancer, achieving strong performance across multiple cohorts.

*Why it matters: Directly addresses computational pathology with AI for cancer diagnosis, combining image analysis with genomic inference for clinical prediction in oncology.*

## [Assessing AI and Neurologist Diagnostic Reasoning Against Neuropathological Ground Truth](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.07.26356930)
**High** · medrxiv · 2026-10-03

Introduces NeuroBench, a dataset of neuropathologically-confirmed neurological cases, and DIAGNO, an LLM-based diagnostic system that achieves performance comparable to neurologists in differential diagnosis with interpretable reasoning.

*Why it matters: Demonstrates LLM application to clinical diagnosis with rigorous evaluation against biological ground truth, advancing AI reasoning methods in healthcare.*
