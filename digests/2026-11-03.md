## [DRAGON-3D: an electron-density-aware generative model for dual-target drug molecule generation.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42935351/)
**High** · pubmed · 2026-10-27

DRAGON-3D is a generative model for dual-target drug molecule design that uses 3D electron-density representations and a bi-Net β-VAE architecture to generate candidate molecules effective against two protein targets simultaneously.

*Why it matters: Directly addresses your interest in generative models for de novo drug design with novel 3D structure-aware approaches and demonstrates how latent-space fusion can improve molecular generation without requiring dual-target training data.*

## [Artificial intelligence-guided engineering of precision ocular therapeutics.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42687624/)
**High** · pubmed · 2026-09-02

A comprehensive review of AI-guided drug discovery and gene therapy for ophthalmic therapeutics, covering foundation models, reinforcement learning, diffusion-based generative models, AlphaFold-based structure prediction, and AI-optimized CRISPR guide RNA design.

*Why it matters: Surveys recent advances across multiple relevant areas including generative models, structure-based drug design, deep learning for regulatory element design, and applications of foundation models to therapeutic discovery.*
