# Paper Digest — 2026-09-28

_Interests: Research on AI/ML methods and their application to healthcare and biology, including: large language models, deep learning architectures, and reasoning/evaluation methods; AI applied to healthcare and electronic health records (EHR), clinical decision support, and clinical NLP; computational pathology and cell/tissue image analysis with AI; genomics and bioinformatics methods, including AI applied to genomic and multi-omics data._

## [Neuro-Symbolic AI for Automated Pathology Quality Measurement](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.22.26358635)
**High** · medrxiv · 2026-09-26

A neuro-symbolic AI system combining large language models with symbolic reasoning achieves high-accuracy automated abstraction of complex pathology quality measures from narrative reports, matching or exceeding trained human abstractors through iterative case-based refinement.

*Why it matters: Directly addresses AI/clinical NLP for healthcare quality measurement and demonstrates effective integration of LLMs with symbolic reasoning for extracting structured clinical information from pathology narratives.*

## [ViSTA: A Simple Bridge Extends Visual Alignment to Clinical Time-Series Understanding in Multimodal LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31448v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-09-25

ViSTA is a compact adapter enabling multimodal LLMs to incorporate irregular clinical time-series data for patient risk prediction and temporal question answering, achieving competitive performance on acute kidney injury and mortality prediction from MIMIC-IV.

*Why it matters: Combines deep learning architectures, LLMs, and clinical EHR data for healthcare prediction tasks, addressing the challenge of integrating structured temporal clinical measurements with language models.*

## [A Structure-Aware Self-Supervised Framework for Transferable Fetal Ultrasound Screening](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.21.26361576)
**High** · medrxiv · 2026-09-27

RadarMAE presents a structure-aware self-supervised learning framework for fetal ultrasound analysis that achieves exceptional diagnostic performance for Down syndrome screening with robust cross-institutional generalization and interpretable risk atlas discovery.

*Why it matters: Demonstrates deep learning for clinical image analysis with application to computational pathology/tissue imaging, including self-supervised methods and explainable AI for medical image interpretation.*

## [Extracting smoking history from clinical notes for lung cancer screening decision support: comparing a structured-judgment model with general-purpose large language models](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.24.26363906)
**High** · medrxiv · 2026-09-26

Compares a structured-judgment model with four general-purpose LLMs for extracting smoking history from clinical notes to support lung cancer screening decision support, showing high accuracy across all systems with tradeoffs in latency and cost.

*Why it matters: Evaluates clinical NLP methods using LLMs for healthcare decision support, providing practical benchmarking of modern language models for structured information extraction from unstructured clinical documentation.*

## [Automated language impairment screening in acute stroke using connected speech](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.14.26360474)
**Medium** · medrxiv · 2026-09-26

Develops an automated discourse screener for acute language impairment in stroke patients using automatic speech recognition and ensemble classifiers combining LLM embeddings with discrete linguistic features, achieving 90% balanced accuracy.

*Why it matters: Applies LLMs and deep learning to clinical assessment automation using connected speech analysis, demonstrating AI for clinical evaluation in acute healthcare settings.*

## [PIA: A Personal Intelligence Agent Turning Health Conversations into Records and Records into Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31255v1)
**Medium** · arxiv · 2026-09-25

PIA is a personal intelligence agent that converts health conversations into structured clinical records and synthesizes understanding from temporal health data, with pluggable modules for schema, medical knowledge graphs, and temporal reasoning.

*Why it matters: Addresses AI/LLM applications to healthcare EHR and clinical data understanding with focus on temporal reasoning and structured clinical record generation from conversational input.*

## [Deep Time-to-Event Models for Intrapartum Fetal Monitoring](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.21.26363416)
**Medium** · medrxiv · 2026-09-26

Develops deep time-to-event models for fetal heart rate monitoring that jointly predict delivery timing and fetal acidosis from continuous CTG signals, reframing the problem as survival analysis rather than assuming known delivery times.

*Why it matters: Applies deep learning architecture (Marked DeepHit) and time-to-event prediction methods to clinical monitoring data, demonstrating novel problem formulation for healthcare AI applications.*

## [Clinical history shapes the predictive value of polygenic risk](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.25.26364029)
**Medium** · medrxiv · 2026-09-27

Using 900,000 UK Biobank and FinnGen participants, develops and validates clinical-genetic prediction models for 150 diseases showing when polygenic risk scores improve prediction beyond clinical data, with largest gains in metabolic and cardiovascular disease.

*Why it matters: Combines AI/ML methods with genomics and multi-omics biobanks to develop disease prediction models, demonstrating application of machine learning to genetic risk assessment and clinical integration.*

## [Validating Artificial Intelligence Guidance for Ultrasound Acquisition and Remote Interpretation](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.16.26356882)
**Medium** · medrxiv · 2026-09-27

A multicenter prospective study validates an AI guidance system for non-expert ultrasound acquisition of lower extremity compression scans, achieving 92.86% sensitivity for DVT detection with 97.96% prioritization specificity.

*Why it matters: Demonstrates practical AI application in clinical imaging for diagnostic decision support, showing how AI guidance can expand access to ultrasound screening in resource-constrained settings.*

## [UniAR: A Unified Framework for Autism Recognition Enhanced by Multi-View Prompt Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31298v1)
**Medium** · arxiv · 2026-09-25

UniAR is a multimodal framework for autism spectrum disorder recognition combining brain MRI and facial analysis with LLM-generated hierarchical diagnostic descriptions and expert-alignment mechanisms, achieving 91.6% accuracy on facial benchmarks.

*Why it matters: Applies deep learning and LLMs to multimodal medical image analysis and clinical decision support for neurodevelopmental disorder screening with semantic reasoning.*

## [Uncertainty-Aware Federated Learning for Infant Movement Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31463v1)
**Low** · arxiv · 2026-09-25

*Why it matters: Combines deep learning architectures with federated learning methods for healthcare AI while addressing privacy constraints, relevant to clinical AI deployment across institutions.*

## [Artificial intelligence in Europe's medical device database: Public traceability of AI-enabled software in EUDAMED.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42800404/)

*Why it matters: Addresses regulatory and transparency issues for AI in healthcare applications, relevant to understanding broader context of AI deployment in medical devices.*
