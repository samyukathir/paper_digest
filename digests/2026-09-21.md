## [Identifying cohorts at elevated risk of cancers using generative modeling of patient health states](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.09.26362676)
**High** · medrxiv · 2026-09-19

GenEHR is an autoregressive generative model trained on EHRs from millions of patients that forecasts disease progression and predicts first cancer diagnosis risk within five years, with potential clinical application for cancer screening prioritization.

*Why it matters: Directly addresses your interests in LLMs/generative models applied to healthcare EHR data and clinical decision support for disease risk prediction.*

## [[Tailoring care to the mind: The emerging role of artificial intelligence in precision psychiatry. Part I: Understanding, measuring and modelling clinical singularity].](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42763257/)
**High** · pubmed · 2026-09-19

Comprehensive review of AI/ML methods in precision psychiatry covering supervised/unsupervised learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, and federated learning applied to multimodal data including EHRs, clinical notes, genomics, neuroimaging, and digital phenotypes.

*Why it matters: Extensively covers multiple AI/ML architectures and their integration with diverse healthcare data types including EHRs, clinical NLP, genomics, and discusses reasoning/evaluation challenges in clinical AI.*

## [Artificial intelligence in onco-anaesthesia: Current applications, challenges, and future directions.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42625971/)
**High** · pubmed · 2026-09-20

Review of AI applications in perioperative cancer care including machine learning for risk stratification from EHR phenotyping, deep learning for closed-loop systems, and multimodal data integration with genomics and wearable biosignals for complication prediction.

*Why it matters: Covers healthcare AI/ML applications to EHR phenotyping, clinical decision support, and integration of genomic data; addresses practical implementation challenges and explainability concerns.*

## [Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in the Context of Hemophilia: A Scope Review.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42765167/)
**Medium** · pubmed · 2026-09-21

Scoping review of AI/ML applications in hemophilia including diagnostic models using CNNs and ensemble methods, and risk prediction based on genetic mutations from F8 gene data for adverse outcome prediction.

*Why it matters: Demonstrates AI/ML applications to healthcare with genomic data (gene mutations) and clinical decision support for disease severity and outcome prediction.*

## [Machine Learning Approach to Identify Gut Microbiota Biomarkers in Patients with ST-Elevation Myocardial Infarction Presenting Primary Ventricular Tachyarrhythmias](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.16.26363271)
**Medium** · medrxiv · 2026-09-20

Machine learning pipeline applied to gut microbiota 16S rRNA sequencing data to identify biomarkers and metabolic pathways associated with primary ventricular arrhythmias in myocardial infarction patients using SVM and functional prediction.

*Why it matters: Applies ML to microbiome/omics data analysis with feature selection and classification for clinical outcome prediction, relevant to your bioinformatics and AI for healthcare interests.*

## [Towards a new psychiatric ontology in the era of artificial intelligence.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.encep.2026.07.003)
**Medium** · europe_pmc · 2026-09-19

Conceptual analysis of how AI might construct new psychiatric ontologies by detecting latent regularities across heterogeneous data including clinical descriptions, digital traces, biomarkers, neuroimaging and genomics, while addressing risks of algorithmic opacity.

*Why it matters: Addresses fundamental epistemological questions about AI/ML reasoning and interpretation in healthcare, particularly multimodal data integration and the gap between statistical patterns and clinical validity.*

## [Generative Artificial Intelligence for Systematic Literature Reviews: A Good Practices Report of an ISPOR Special Task Force.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42763092/)
**Low** · pubmed · 2026-09-19

ISPOR task force report providing evidence-based recommendations for responsible use of generative AI tools across systematic literature review workflows with emphasis on human-in-the-loop oversight and avoiding autonomous deployment.

*Why it matters: While focused on literature reviews rather than primary clinical/biological research, provides relevant guidance on evaluating and implementing GenAI systems responsibly in healthcare evidence synthesis.*
