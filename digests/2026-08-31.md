# Paper Digest — 2026-08-31

## [A Pragmatic Randomized Trial of an EHR-Integrated Generative AI Chart Summarization Tool for Ambulatory Clinicians](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.26.26361496)
**High** · medrxiv · 2026-08-31

A pragmatic randomized controlled trial evaluating an EHR-integrated generative AI chart summarization tool across 284 clinicians, showing modest reductions in physician task load and burnout despite declining engagement and user concerns about accuracy.

*Why it matters: Directly addresses AI/ML application to clinical workflows and EHR systems with rigorous RCT methodology, demonstrating real-world implementation challenges of generative AI in healthcare.*

## [Balancing privacy and performance: the impact of facial defacing on AI in medical imaging.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2026.106457)
**High** · europe_pmc · 2026-08-29

Systematic evaluation of facial defacing algorithms on medical imaging AI tasks, quantifying the privacy-utility trade-off and demonstrating that facial replacement methods like mri_reface preserve model performance while maintaining patient privacy.

*Why it matters: Addresses computational pathology and medical image analysis with deep learning, examining practical deployment considerations for AI-ready medical imaging datasets.*

## [AGENT-O: A Semantic Agent Card Framework for Interoperable and Governed Healthcare AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28345v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-08-28

AGENT-O is a semantic ontology framework and Agent Card for representing and assessing healthcare AI agent systems, revealing significant gaps in reporting of runtime architecture, governance, and reproducibility across 279 published papers.

*Why it matters: Directly relevant to AI/ML methods applied to healthcare, providing structured evaluation and reporting frameworks for healthcare AI agents with emphasis on governance and reproducibility.*

## [ARC-CT: Anatomy-Routed Contrastive Vision-Language Learning for 3D Chest CT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28455v1)
**High** · arxiv · 2026-08-28

ARC-CT proposes an anatomy-routed contrastive vision-language learning framework for 3D chest CT that uses LLM-extracted labels and region-aware learning to improve abnormality classification without manual annotations.

*Why it matters: Combines deep learning, vision-language models, and clinical NLP for medical image analysis; demonstrates novel approaches to weakly-supervised learning in computational pathology.*

## [Artificial intelligence in Latin American hepatology: current evidence, implementation barriers, and strategic priorities.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aohep.2026.102432)
**Medium** · europe_pmc · 2026-08-30

A comprehensive narrative review of AI applications in Latin American hepatology, covering fibrosis risk stratification, digital pathology, and electrocardiogram-based screening, with emphasis on implementation barriers and phased adoption strategies.

*Why it matters: Reviews AI applications across healthcare including clinical decision support and digital pathology, with practical insights on implementation in resource-limited settings.*

## [Machine learning analysis of Autism phenotype data supports a four-dimensional continuum with three overlapping subtypes](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.27.26361561)
**Medium** · medrxiv · 2026-08-31

Machine learning analysis using Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) on autism phenotype data from 36,710 individuals, identifying a four-dimensional continuum model with three overlapping subtypes and demonstrating improved accuracy over traditional statistical approaches.

*Why it matters: Demonstrates deep learning application to large-scale clinical phenotypic data with novel dimensionality reduction for personalized diagnosis and intervention.*

## [Anatomy-Aware Promptable Segmentation with Online Interactive Training for AUTOPET V](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28461v1)
**Medium** · arxiv · 2026-08-28

Anatomy-aware promptable segmentation model for whole-body lesion detection in FDG and PSMA PET/CT using nnU-Net with interactive training and anatomical context through organ supervision.

*Why it matters: Addresses computational pathology and tissue image analysis using deep learning with organ-level anatomical constraints for improved clinical segmentation.*

## [BanglaMed-QA: A Question Answering System for Healthcare Support in Bangla](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28329v1)
**Low** · arxiv · 2026-08-28

BanglaMed-QA is a question-answering system for medical domain in Bangla, combining supervised machine learning (SVM) with multiple similarity metrics for a low-resource language healthcare QA task.

*Why it matters: Demonstrates NLP applied to clinical decision support in healthcare, though limited to a single non-English language with traditional machine learning rather than modern deep learning approaches.*
